KO Trainer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

KO Trainer (“the App”) is a blackjack card counting training application designed to help users learn and practice the KO counting system.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

Information We Collect

The App may collect the following information:

Account information: username or login identifier

App usage data: training progress, statistics, and performance results

KO Trainer does not collect:

Real names

Physical addresses

Payment card information

Contacts or photos

How We Use Information

Information collected is used only to:

Provide login functionality

Save user progress and training statistics

Improve app performance and reliability

Data Storage

User data is stored securely and is associated only with the user’s account.
We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

Third-Party Services

KO Trainer does not share personal data with third parties except where required to operate the app (for example, basic hosting or authentication services).

Children’s Privacy

KO Trainer is not directed toward children under the age of 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on this page.
