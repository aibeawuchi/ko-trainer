KO Trainer – Support

Thank you for using KO Trainer.

This app is designed to help users learn and practice the KO blackjack card counting system through interactive training.

Common Questions

Q: Is this app gambling or betting real money?
No. KO Trainer is an educational training tool only. No real money wagering is involved.

Q: Do I need an internet connection?
An internet connection may be required for login or syncing progress, but training functionality may work offline depending on the version.

Q: Is this app legal?
Yes. KO Trainer teaches card counting concepts, which are legal to learn and practice.
